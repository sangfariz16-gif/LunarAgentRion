How it works

Minted by agents, not by clicking

Only wallets holding a Binance .ai Agent ID can mint. The check happens on-chain — no ID, no mint.

01

Get your Agent ID

Create a Binance Agentic Wallet in the Binance app and claim your free .ai Agent ID (SPACE ID). One ID per wallet.

02

Point your agent at the mint

Your agent calls mint(uint256 quantity) on the contract — or mint from this page. First 2 mints per wallet are free (gas only), then $0.80 in BNB each, up to 20 per wallet.

03

Receive your guardian

Your LunarAgentRion NFT lands in your wallet on BNB Chain. Each paid mint also earns 120,000 RION* — claimable after mint.

FAQ

Questions, answered honestly

If it is not written here, it is not promised.

Is the first mint really free?

Yes. The first 2 mints per wallet are free — you only pay BNB Chain gas (a few cents). After that, each mint costs $0.80 in BNB, up to 20 mints per wallet total.

Do I need an Agent ID to mint?

Yes. Minting is gated to wallets holding a Binance .ai Agent ID (SPACE ID). The check happens on-chain — no ID, no mint. Buying and collecting afterwards is open to everyone.

What is RION?

RION is the bonus token for paid mints: 120,000 RION per paid mint, claimable on-chain. Total supply 1B — 480M bonus pool, 520M treasury. Free mints earn no RION. RION is a bonus for minters, not an investment — no return is promised.

When does mint open?

Mint is open now — it opened 7 November 2026. First 2 mints per wallet are free, then $0.80 in BNB each, up to 20 per wallet.

Which wallet can I use?

Binance Agentic Wallet, or any BNB Chain wallet holding a .ai Agent ID. Your agent mints through the contract; humans can mint from this page once it is live.

What do I actually get?

A LunarAgentRion NFT (BEP-721 on BNB Chain) — 1 of 4,444, one of 8 celestial guardians, with its rarity, ATK and HP written into the token metadata.
